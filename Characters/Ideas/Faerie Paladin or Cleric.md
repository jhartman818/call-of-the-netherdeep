

## Theme
*Expectations vs Identity*.  This character's underlying theme is around identity in the face of societal expectations.

I like the juxtaposition between the beliefs and rules that faeries are expected to follow vs. those that would be required to be a devout follower of a LG deity.
I also like the juxtaposition of a faerie who willingly negates the use of their wings in order to don armor that represents their deity (even if they do occasionally take it off to accomplish something).

## Surface Demeanor
Mischievous but devout.  A bit tired of explaining themselves, but undeterred in following their own path.  Occasionally upset with themselves for allowing their unique personality from ever truly fitting in either the Faerie box or the Paladin/Cleric box.

## Backstory Overview
There are lots of possibilities on how, but in some manner, the family of this particular Faerie travelled to the material plane, had an encounter, and were saved by a passing band of adventurers, at least one of which was a cleric or paladin.  At this time, the faerie knew that its purpose -- like being exposed to the word for something that you've known forever.  Probably left their family on the spot to go search out a temple of the deity and eventually worked up to cleric- and/or paladin-hood.
