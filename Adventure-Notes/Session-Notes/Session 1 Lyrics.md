# Five Voices, One Song

**[Intro — layered harmonies]**  
Oh-oh-oh, oh-oh-oh…  
Oh baby, baby…  
Feels like we found our people.

**[Verse 1]**  
Two were chasing memories, two were chasing something new,  
One came to the festival with orders to see through.  
Dreams of ink and demons, roads that stretched so far—  
Funny how you find your people finding where you are.

Warren had his brother’s back, a cape against the breeze,  
Vaeroth carried questions underneath those tattooed sleeves.  
Bert came with a challenge, Frostbane with a blade—  
Five different beginnings, one connection being made.

**[Pre-Chorus]**  
One laugh, one look, one terrible plan,  
Somehow everybody understands.  
Came here with a reason, found a reason we could stay—  
Everybody’s got somebody at the end of the day.

**[Chorus]**  
Oh-oh-oh, we’re better when we’re together,  
Oh baby, baby, get a little closer now.  
Five different voices never sounded any better,  
Hear us coming through the streets of Jigow.  
Throw your hands up, let the good times roll,  
Got a little more courage when you’re not alone.  
Oh-oh-oh, whatever comes along—  
We’ve got five voices, but we’re singing one song.

**[Verse 2]**  
Gleb and Warren found a turkey, skipped the part where people pay,  
Vilarus picked up the bill and kept us on our way.  
Vendors calling, river crowds, a whole town shining bright,  
We were learning everybody’s kind of trouble that night.

Bert went down arm-wrestling; the betting didn’t pay,  
Then Vaeroth turned into an ape and took a win away.  
One big medal, plenty lost, a story we could share—  
You don’t need to win them all when all your friends are there.

**[Pre-Chorus]**  
One cheer, one groan, one “Try that again!”  
Getting pretty good at being friends.  
Didn’t plan to need each other walking through that gate—  
Now there’s always someone saying, “Hey, we’ll wait.”

**[Chorus]**  
Oh-oh-oh, we’re better when we’re together,  
Oh baby, baby, get a little closer now.  
Five different voices never sounded any better,  
Hear us coming through the streets of Jigow.  
Throw your hands up, let the good times roll,  
Got a little more courage when you’re not alone.  
Oh-oh-oh, whatever comes along—  
We’ve got five voices, but we’re singing one song.

**[Bridge — heartfelt breakdown]**  
When the Watch got heavy, somebody stood their ground,  
When a debt got ugly, two brothers gathered round.  
Fourteen twenty-eight in gold is quite a weight to bear—  
Feels a little different when somebody says, “We care.”

Now we’ve got a starry map, a lockbox and some clues,  
Seven colored bottles we should probably think through.  
An inn upon a turtle, Agathe’s pies to try—  
We haven’t got the answers, but we’ve got all night.

**[Final Chorus — key change, absolutely mandatory]**  
OH-OH-OH, WE’RE BETTER WHEN WE’RE TOGETHER!  
Oh baby, baby, can you feel it now?  
Five different voices never sounded any better,  
Raising up the rooftops all around Jigow.  
Win a little glory, make a few mistakes,  
Save a seat beside you, whatever road we take.  
Oh-oh-oh, whatever comes along—  
We’ve got five voices, but we’re singing one song.

**[Outro — overlapping ad-libs]**  
Oh-oh-oh… *(Save a place for me…)*  
Oh baby, baby… *(Right here with the team…)*  
Different roads, but this is where we belong—  
Five voices.  
One song.
