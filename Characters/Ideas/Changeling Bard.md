

## Theme
*What is a soul*.  This character's underlying theme is whether a created construct can have a meaningful existence without a soul.

## Surface Demeanor
Unbridled optimism.  Driven to shine a light on hope and valor everywhere they go -- exposing it and bringing it out of people around them, or regaling stories of it from long ago.  Can be intensely curious about other people, until it seems unlikely to unveil new stories, and then sometimes too willing to take the spotlight to make sure hopeful stories fill the air.  Deftly avoids talking about themselves during these stories.

## Backstory Overview
The character is actually a construct with the ability to shape change (using changeling species rules), built using motes of the essences of hope and valor, but built by an evil archmage as a tool to sow laziness and complacence in a rival kingdom (nearest parallel might be Hayt/Duncan Idaho's golem in Dune Messiah and Children of Dune).  The character succeeded, and the kingdom fell, but instead of being reclaimed by the archmage and used in further nefarious plots, the bard escaped.  Having laid low for long enough, the construct cannot help but venture forth to share.
