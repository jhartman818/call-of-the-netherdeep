## Active Quests

### Main Quest Notes

#### Gleb and Warren's Reason for Traveling to Jigow

- The brothers were asked to go to Jigow "to stop the destruction of the town."
- **Status:** Both have reached Jigow. The source and details of the request have not been recorded.

#### Alyxian's Plea and the Jewel of Three Prayers

- After touching an orb in the Emerald Grotto, the party experienced a vision of a shackled human named **Alyxian**. His name spelling is confirmed in Session 3.
- He invoked the Moonweaver, spoke of having prayed to the Changebringer in "this temple of evil," and asked the party to take his jewel.
- Session 3 ended mid-request at "I beg you, take my jewel and..." The rest of his plea has not been recorded.
- In Session 4, the party identified the **Jewel of Three Prayers**, a **Vestige of Divergence** from the **Calamity**. Its recorded properties are invisibility, light, a **+1 bonus to the wearer's AC**, and **3 charges**; charge costs and recharge details are available in the Foundry item details.
- **New vision:** Ushu (name as recorded; identity and spelling unconfirmed) saw an amulet rise from blood. Many hands reached for it, and a handsome, curly-haired man took it and said, "they must find me, the ones from the grotto." Whether this man was Alyxian is unconfirmed.
- **Current objective:** The party has been tasked with heading to **Bazzoxan** to speak with **Verin Thelyss**, the **taskhand**. The meeting has not been recorded.
- Session 4 mentions a possible race against Ayo's crew, but no agreement or details were confirmed.

- **Open thread:** Vaeroth had a vision involving Bert's army, then awoke in Bazzoxan with Bert over him.
- The two are staying together because they may have answers to each other's riddles.
- **New lead:** Alyxian, the shackled human in the vision encountered after touching an orb in the Emerald Grotto, seemed familiar to Bert, but Bert did not know why.
- **Status:** The connection between Bert and Vaeroth remains unexplained. Any link to the figure in the vision is unconfirmed.

## Completed Quests

### Side Quest Notes

#### Bert's Arm-Wrestling Challenge

- Salaria and Evan, low-ranking soldiers in Bazzoxan, "ordered" Bert to travel to Jigow and challenge Maryl Bronzefang to arm wrestling.
- **Status:** Completed. The challenge obligation is resolved because Bert no longer needs to work for the Aurora Watch. Bert competed and lost badly; its opponent was not identified in the notes.

#### Pie-Eating Contest

- Gleb and Warren entered the contest featuring pies made by Agathe Silverspoon.
- **Status:** Completed. Gleb may have won, but the result is unconfirmed. Warren needed healing afterward.

#### Festival of Merit: Emerald Grotto Final Challenge

- Two teams were chosen at the fireworks gathering to race through the Emerald Grotto.
- Ayo's team was selected first; the party was selected second, led by Vilarus.
- **Objective:** Retrieve an amulet hidden in the grotto, matching the gold-and-emerald amulet displayed at the briefing, and return it to the podium.
- **Announced reward:** 100 gp each and the title **Champions of Merit**.
- **Progress:** Vilarus removed a spear from a shark's side, then cut a rope to free the challenge amulet from it. The party later followed Ayo's team up a secret passage to an orb and experienced a vision.
- **Status:** Completed. The party returned the challenge amulet to the podium and received the monetary reward.
