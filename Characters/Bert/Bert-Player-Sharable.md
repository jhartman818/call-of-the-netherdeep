# Bert - Player Sharable

![Bert body-design reference](<./Bert-Body-Reference.jpg>)

## Quick Concept

Bert is a sentient automaton wizard using warforged rules and it/its pronouns. Its formal military designation is **SC-AFS-3-6**, meaning **Support Construct - Arcane Fire-Support - Version 3 - Serial 6**, formally pronounced **"Skafis Three-Six."**

It had no official military rank. Its operational role was arcane fire-support specialist, and commanders addressed it as Three-Six or "the support construct." Soldiers joked that one construct providing their arcane artillery amounted to an entire **battery**. Battery eventually shortened to **Bert**, and squadmates sometimes used **Specialist Bert** as an affectionate, unofficial title.

## Appearance

Bert has a lean, long-limbed frame built from utilitarian metal components. Simple silver-gray plates cover exposed dark machinery at the neck, abdomen, and compact hinged limb joints. Its smooth yellow faceplate has small, dark, button-like optical sensors and no expressive human features.

Bert stands 5 feet 6 inches tall and weighs approximately 240 pounds. Its exact age is unknown, but it remembers a little more than a decade of active life.

The armor is weathered but intact, with restrained scratches, shallow dents, rivets, and repair seams. An angular yellow hawk-head insignia marks its chest. A small yellow armor patch is riveted over one thigh plate; the patch is a repair rather than a second insignia.

## Personality

Bert is literal, practical, and difficult to read. Its dry military humor is often indistinguishable from sincere belief. It naturally describes an adventuring group as a squad and evaluates plans in terms of roles, coverage, risk, and operational usefulness.

It is comfortable offering occasional suggestions but avoids giving orders. Bert informally recognizes situational party leadership and acts independently and tactically during combat unless another character demonstrates reliable tactical leadership.

Off duty, Bert slouches, sits around, and avoids unnecessary exertion. It enjoys gambling. When combat begins, the laziness disappears and it becomes completely professional.

## Wizardry

Bert is an Evocation wizard that treats magic as arcane artillery rather than academic theory. It uses normal wizard spellbook, preparation, copying, and spell-learning mechanics.

Its spellbook is a well-made but otherwise ordinary leatherbound tome with **SC-AFS-3-6** embossed in the upper-right corner of the front cover.

Some of Bert's magical habits came from ordinary soldiers. For example, it was taught that *Fire Bolt* works better if accompanied by finger guns and that *Fireball* should be called "serving breakfast" so the enemy does not know what is coming.

## Service and Restoration

Bert served during the Calamity. It remembers daily life with its old squad vividly, including the downtime, the jokes, and the slang, but its memories of the fighting are fragmented. It was disabled during its final battle and lay buried for centuries.

Scholars from the Allegiance of Allsight excavated and restored Bert about eighteen months before the campaign. Bert is working off its restoration debt as an Allegiance field agent. The Allegiance sends it to investigate historical battle sites when its memories return, and Bert takes other adventuring work between assignments. Bert is happy to tell stories about its old squad but says little about how its service ended.

Any connection between Bert and another party member from before the group formed will be worked out in Session 0.

## Existing Party History

The campaign begins with the characters already established as an adventuring group. We have worked together several times over approximately the past year and have just completed a thirteen-day transport job from Asarius to Jigow.

Bert already treats the group as a squad-like unit. The details of how those relationships began are left for the players to develop together.
